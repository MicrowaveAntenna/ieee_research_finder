表里能直接看出一条主线：剖面越低，带宽越窄。0.173λ 时带宽是 54.8%，降到 0.097λ₀ 时只剩 28.2%。而且前者用的是更严格的 SWR≤1.5 判据，按同一判据比较，差距还会更大。

近几年的工作主要在想办法把低剖面下丢掉的带宽补回来，思路是引入额外的谐振点：

  • 电容加载环（CLL）加开槽短截线：TAP 2025
  • 折叠辐射臂加不等长臂：AWPL 2025
  • 碎片化领结形电偶极子：TAP 2025
  • 开槽电偶极子：AWPL 2026 early access，这篇还没有摘要，只能看标题

同时实现"宽带"和"剖面低于 0.1λ₀"仍然比较难，这正好可以作为论文的切入点。

可以直接改写的综述段落（英文草稿）

Owing to the complementary combination of an electric dipole and a magnetic dipole, the magneto-electric (ME) dipole exhibits a wide impedance bandwidth, stable gain, and low back radiation [1]. Impedance bandwidths of 95.2% and even 110%–114% have been achieved with bowtie electric dipoles and improved feeding structures [2]–[4]. However, the original ME dipole has a profile of about 0.25λ0 [5]. By folding the vertical shorted patches, the profile was reduced to 0.173λ, 0.169λ, and 0.116λ0 [5]–[7], and an obtuse-triangular magnetic dipole further lowered it to 0.097λ0 [8]. Substrate-integrated designs, in which metallic vias serve as the magnetic dipole, reach about 0.1λ with a 40% bandwidth [9], and metasurface loading enables a profile of 0.058λ0 [10]. Nevertheless, the bandwidth generally decreases as the profile is reduced, e.g., from 54.8% (SWR ≤ 1.5) at 0.173λ [5] to 28.2% (SWR < 2) at 0.097λ0 [8]. To recover the bandwidth, recent works introduce additional resonances through capacitively loaded loops and stub-added notches [11], unequal-length folded arms [12], and fragmented or slotted electric dipoles [13], [14].
