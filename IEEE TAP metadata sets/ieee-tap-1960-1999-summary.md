## IRE vs IEEE title (1960–1962)

IRE TAP (vols. 8–10, 1960–1962) is the direct predecessor of IEEE TAP and shares IEEE Xplore punumber 8. Crossref indexes these papers under ISSN **0096-1973** with container-title **IRE Transactions on Antennas and Propagation**. Those records are cited with the Crossref container-title verbatim. From vol. 11 (January 1963) Crossref’s container-title is IEEE Transactions on Antennas and Propagation (even when the DOI sits on the IRE ISSN backfile). No titles were invented.

## Data sources

1. **Crossref REST API**  
   - `https://api.crossref.org/journals/0018-926X/works` (IEEE TAP print ISSN)  
   - `https://api.crossref.org/journals/0096-1973/works` (IRE TAP print ISSN; holds the 1960–1987 backfile, including IEEE-era 1963–1987 papers that are **not** on 0018-926X)  
   Filter: `from-pub-date:1960-01-01,until-pub-date:1999-12-31`. Cursor pagination, `rows=1000`, no `select=` (the `link` field is required for real IEEE arnumbers). Merged by DOI.  
   A fuzzy `query.container-title` search was **not** used for the IRE title: it matches hundreds of thousands of unrelated works.  
   Used for bibliographic metadata: authors, title, volume, issue, pages, publication date, DOI, container-title, and IEEE Xplore arnumber.  
   Crossref records in dump: **8930**. Records with abstracts: **0**. Records with `subject` keywords: **0**. Records with `link`: **8930**.  
   ISSN values seen on records: 0018-926X: 4058, 0096-1973: 4872, 1558-2221: 1167, 1558-3643: 18.

2. **Semantic Scholar Academic Graph API**  
   - Bulk search by venue (IEEE TAP and IRE TAP, years 1960–1999) is thin for this era (**15** papers, **1** with abstracts).  
   - Paper batch lookup by DOI for Crossref records (**8249** hits, **197** with abstracts). Publisher-elided abstracts are treated as missing.  
   Catalog abstracts from Semantic Scholar: **195**; from Crossref: **0**.  
   Older years have sparse abstracts — expected; `Abstract: N/A` is written rather than fabricating.

3. **IEEE Xplore stamp URLs** use the real article number extracted from Crossref `link[].URL` (`arnumber=` query param or `/NNNNNN.pdf` path), falling back to `resource.primary.URL` path `/document/{id}`. The IEEE DOI suffix is **not** assumed to be the Xplore arnumber. If no arnumber is present, the URL falls back to `https://doi.org/{DOI}`.  
   arnumber from `link`: 8547; from `resource.primary`: 0; from `alternative-id`: 0.

## Filtering (administrative items excluded)

From the 8930 Crossref records, items were dropped when they were clearly not research content:

- Table of contents, covers (including `[Front cover and table of contents]`, `[Back cover]`, pages C1–C4), publication information, information for authors/contributors, editorial boards, institutional listings, indexes, contributors pages, frontispiece, blank pages
- Publisher ads, special-issue banner pages, calls for papers, society award announcements, in-memoriam notes, and other items with no authors
- Records whose print volume is outside 8–47 (not 1960–1999 TAP volumes)
- Records whose issued/print year is outside 1960–1999
- Volume/year mismatches greater than 2 years vs. TAP rule volume ≈ year − 1952

Skipped counts from this build: {'admin': 383}

Semantic Scholar items with no matching Crossref DOI were **not** added; Crossref DOI is the catalog key.

## Known gaps

- **Abstracts:** 8352 catalog papers have `Abstract: N/A` because neither Crossref nor Semantic Scholar provided an abstract (IEEE typically does not deposit TAP abstracts in Crossref; S2 often elides publisher-copyrighted abstracts for this backfile). Abstracts were **not** fabricated.
- **Keywords:** OpenAlex topic keywords were added where available. IEEE author keywords / IEEE Thesaurus terms are generally not in Crossref/OpenAlex; 8544 records now have OpenAlex topics, 3 remain `keywords: {N/A}`.
- **Month names** use Crossref `published-print` / `issued` when a month is present. Issue-number→month mapping is applied **only** for volumes that actually have 12 monthly issues in Crossref. Bimonthly/quarterly volumes never get a guessed month from the issue number.
- Author names are converted to IEEE initials + family name from Crossref given/family fields.
- Extra issue labels (parts/combined/special): ['vol. 12 no. 7'].
- Crossref coverage for this journal **is** the catalog: vols. 8–47 are all present in the dump. Empty-looking years were not invented.
