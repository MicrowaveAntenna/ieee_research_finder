## Data sources

1. **Crossref REST API** (`https://api.crossref.org/journals/0018-926X/works`)  
   Filter: `from-pub-date:2000-01-01,until-pub-date:2020-12-31`. Cursor pagination, `rows=1000`, no `select=` (the `link` field is required for real IEEE arnumbers).  
   Used for bibliographic metadata: authors, title, volume, issue, pages, publication date, DOI, and IEEE Xplore arnumber.  
   Crossref records in dump: **14127**. Records with abstracts: **0**. Records with `subject` keywords: **0**. Records with `link`: **14093**.

2. **Semantic Scholar Academic Graph API**  
   - Bulk search: venue = IEEE Transactions on Antennas and Propagation, years 2000–2020 (**11396** papers, **2948** with abstracts).  
   - Paper batch lookup by DOI for Crossref records still missing abstracts (**9657** hits, **56** with abstracts).  
   Catalog abstracts from Semantic Scholar: **2846**; from Crossref: **0**.

3. **IEEE Xplore stamp URLs** use the real article number extracted from Crossref `link[].URL` (`arnumber=` query param), falling back to `resource.primary.URL` path `/document/{id}`. The IEEE DOI suffix is **not** the Xplore arnumber (e.g. DOI `10.1109/TAP.2020.3030907` → arnumber `9234023`). If no arnumber is present, the URL falls back to `https://doi.org/{DOI}`.  
   arnumber from `link`: 12606; from `resource.primary`: 3; from `alternative-id`: 0.

## Filtering (administrative items excluded)

From the 14127 Crossref records, items were dropped when they were clearly not research content:

- Table of contents, covers (pages C1–C4), publication information, information for authors, editorial boards, institutional listings
- Publisher ads (TechRxiv, IEEE Open Access, IEEE Collabratec)
- Special-issue banner pages, calls for papers, society award announcements, in-memoriam notes, and other single-page items with no authors
- Crossref “ghost” records that are reprints of much older papers (DOI year before 2000) with no volume in 48–68
- Records whose print volume is outside 48–68 (not 2000–2020 TAP volumes)
- Records whose issued/print year is outside 2000–2020

Skipped counts from this build: {'admin': 1517, 'non_tap': 1}

Semantic Scholar items with TechRxiv/arXiv-only DOIs were **not** added; Crossref DOI is the catalog key.

## Known gaps

- **Abstracts:** 9763 catalog papers have `Abstract: N/A` because neither Crossref nor Semantic Scholar provided an abstract. IEEE typically does not deposit TAP abstracts in Crossref.
- **Keywords:** OpenAlex topic keywords were added where available. IEEE author keywords / IEEE Thesaurus terms are generally not in Crossref/OpenAlex; 12603 records now have OpenAlex topics, 6 remain `keywords: {N/A}`. Filling IEEE thesaurus keywords would require IEEE Xplore (paywalled / bot-blocked).
- Month names use Crossref `published-print` when a month is present; otherwise the issue number of this monthly journal is mapped to the English month name (no. 1 = January, …). Combined / part issues keep the Crossref issue string and may have no mapped month.
- Author names are converted to IEEE initials + family name from Crossref given/family fields. Hyphenated given names become forms like `G.-Y. Deng`.
- Extra issue labels (parts/combined): none beyond 1–12.
