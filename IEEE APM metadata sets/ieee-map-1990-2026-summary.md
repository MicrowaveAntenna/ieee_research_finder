2. **Semantic Scholar Academic Graph API**  
   - Bulk search: venue = IEEE Antennas and Propagation Magazine, years 1990–2026 (**2938** papers, **632** with abstracts).  
   - Paper batch lookup by DOI for Crossref records still missing abstracts (**2164** hits, **12** with abstracts).  
   Catalog abstracts from Semantic Scholar: **517**; from Crossref: **0**.  
   Semantic Scholar-only preprints were **not** added; Crossref DOI is the catalog key.

3. **IEEE Xplore stamp URLs** use the real article number extracted from Crossref `link[].URL` (`arnumber=` query param, or `/ielxN/punumber/isnumber/arnumber.pdf` path), falling back to `resource.primary.URL` path `/document/{id}`. The IEEE DOI suffix is **not** the Xplore arnumber (e.g. DOI `10.1109/MAP.2025.3555843` → arnumber `10962214`, isnumber `11657545`). If no arnumber is present, the URL falls back to `https://doi.org/{DOI}`.

## Filtering (this is a MAGAZINE, not a transactions journal)

From the 7805 Crossref records, items were dropped when they were not authored technical/feature content:

- **Administrative (1514):** covers (C1–C4), table of contents, masthead, magazine staff, publication information, information for authors/contributors, institutional listings, IEEE copyright page, ads, blank pages, membership applications, address-change/air-freight notices, IEEE product ads (TAP/AWPL/Access/OJAP)
- **Society news / standing columns (2495):** Editor's comments, President's message, From the Screen of Stone, Chapter news, AP-S Turnstile, Hidden Word, Meetings & Symposia, Courses, Distinguished Lecturers, AdCom minutes/rosters, society officer lists, Ethically Speaking, Cope Corner, IEEE-USA, Women in Engineering, Young Professionals, In Memoriam, awards announcements, Et Cetera, Poet's Corner, job openings, guest editorials, Testing Ourselves quizzes
- **Column headers / 1-page column shells (11):** department name only (e.g. “AMTA Corner”, “Education Column”) or 1-page foreword/solicitation without a technical article title
- **1-page non-technical with authors (55):** 1-page society news/rosters that happened to carry an author
- **No authors (1011):** items with no Crossref (or S2) authors after the above filters
- Technical standing columns (**Antenna Designer's Notebook, EM Programmer's Notebook, Measurements Corner, Wireless Corner, Antenna Applications Corner, Education Column/Corner, Historical Corner, AMTA Corner, Book Review, Open Problems in CEM**, etc.) were **kept** when they have authors and a real technical/review/historical title (typically spanning more than 1 page)
- When in doubt: **kept** items with authors + a technical title + pages spanning more than 1 page

Letters/comments/replies/corrections **with authors and a technical target** are included. Book reviews of named works are included.

## Known gaps

- **Abstracts:** 2202 catalog papers have `Abstract: N/A` because neither Crossref nor Semantic Scholar provided an abstract. IEEE typically does not deposit MAP abstracts in Crossref (Crossref abstracts in dump: 0).
- **Keywords:** OpenAlex topic keywords were added where available. IEEE author keywords / IEEE Thesaurus terms are generally not in Crossref/OpenAlex; 2714 records now have OpenAlex topics, 5 remain `keywords: {N/A}`. Filling IEEE thesaurus keywords would require IEEE Xplore (paywalled / bot-blocked).
- Month names use Crossref `published-print` when a month is present, abbreviated IEEE-style (Jan., Feb., Mar., Apr., May, June, July, Aug., Sept., Oct., Nov., Dec.). Some early `10.1109/74.*` records are year-only (no month).
- Author names are converted to IEEE initials + family name from Crossref given/family fields. Three or more authors use “A, B and C” **without** an Oxford comma.
- **Date cutoff:** papers with print/issued month after August 2026, or volume 68 issue number &gt; 4, are excluded.
- Early 1990s Crossref coverage is thinner than later years (the Magazine existed, but fewer records were deposited). The catalog reports the actual Crossref grid; it does not invent missing issues.
- 11 Crossref records have no volume (Early Access / unassigned); those that survived filtering are listed as early access.
