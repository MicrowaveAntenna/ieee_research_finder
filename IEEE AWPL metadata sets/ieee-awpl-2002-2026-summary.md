## Data sources

1. **Crossref REST API** (`https://api.crossref.org/journals/1536-1225/works` and `.../1548-5757/works`)  
   Filter: `from-pub-date:2002-01-01,until-pub-date:2026-08-28`. Cursor pagination, `rows=1000`, **no `select=`** (the `link` field is required for real IEEE arnumbers and isnumbers). Records merged by DOI.  
   Used for bibliographic metadata: authors, title, volume, issue, pages, publication date, DOI, arnumber, isnumber.  
   Crossref records in dump: **11763**. Records with abstracts: **1**. Records with `subject` keywords: **0**. Records with `link`: **11757**.

2. **Semantic Scholar Academic Graph API**  
   - Bulk search: venue = IEEE Antennas and Wireless Propagation Letters, years 2002–2026 (**11337** papers, **5848** with abstracts).  
   - Paper batch lookup by DOI for Crossref records still missing abstracts (**5462** hits, **3** with abstracts).  
   Catalog abstracts from Semantic Scholar: **5843**; from Crossref: **1**.  
   Semantic Scholar-only preprints were **not** added; Crossref DOI is the catalog key.

3. **IEEE Xplore stamp URLs** use the real article number extracted from Crossref `link[].URL` (`arnumber=` query param, or `/ielxN/punumber/isnumber/arnumber.pdf` path), falling back to `resource.primary.URL` path `/document/{id}`. The IEEE DOI suffix is **not** the Xplore arnumber (e.g. DOI `10.1109/LAWP.2026.3690040` → arnumber `11505690`, isnumber `11643432`). If no arnumber is present, the URL falls back to `https://doi.org/{DOI}`.  
   arnumber from `link`: 11386; from `resource.primary`: 6; from `alternative-id`: 0.  
   isnumber present on 11386 catalog papers.

## Filtering (administrative items excluded)

From the 11763 Crossref records, items were dropped when they were clearly not research content:

- Table of contents, covers (pages C1–C4), publication information, information for authors, editorial boards, institutional listings
- Publisher ads (TechRxiv, IEEE Open Access, IEEE Collabratec)
- Reviewer lists, award pages, blank pages
- Special-issue banner pages, calls for papers, society award announcements, in-memoriam notes, and other items with no authors
- Records whose issued/print year is outside 2002–2026
- Volume 25 issues after August 2026 (date cutoff)

Letters, comments, replies, and corrections/errata **with authors** are included.

## Known gaps

- **Abstracts:** 5548 catalog papers have `Abstract: N/A` because neither Crossref nor Semantic Scholar provided an abstract. IEEE typically does not deposit AWPL abstracts in Crossref.
- **Keywords:** OpenAlex topic keywords were added where available. IEEE author keywords / IEEE Thesaurus terms are generally not in Crossref/OpenAlex; 11353 records now have OpenAlex topics, 39 remain `keywords: {N/A}`. Filling IEEE thesaurus keywords would require IEEE Xplore (paywalled / bot-blocked).
- Month names use Crossref `published-print` when a month is present, abbreviated IEEE-style (Jan., Feb., Mar., Apr., May, June, July, Aug., Sept., Oct., Nov., Dec.). Early annual volumes often have year-only dates and **no issue number** (the `, no. Y` clause is omitted).
- Author names are converted to IEEE initials + family name from Crossref given/family fields. Three or more authors use “A, B and C” **without** an Oxford comma.
- **Date cutoff:** papers with print/issued month after August 2026, or volume 25 issue number &gt; 8, are excluded.
